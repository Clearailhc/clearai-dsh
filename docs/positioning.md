# ClearAI Positioning

> **Being rebuilt.** This document describes the target design from the ["less is more" plan](less-is-more-plan.zh-CN.md).

## What it is

ClearAI is a **DSH-native ontology discovery and exploration platform**: AI grows a living **domain ontology** in a real project — the project's own vocabulary, conclusions established through the loop, and their graph. The epistemic loop is how the ontology is produced: every judgement states what would prove it wrong, goes through a test that could fail, and becomes a bounded conclusion on evidence before it grows into the ontology.

The dividing line: other knowledge graphs pile up edges by extraction; here **every edge has to be earned through the loop**. The ontology adjudicates nothing; it only houses what has been adjudicated.

ClearAI is not an autonomous truth machine and makes no recursive self-improvement (RSI) claim. It helps people reason better without replacing their judgement.

## Who it is for

People working on problems with conflicting sources, incomplete evidence or no clear path to a solution: researchers, engineers, analysts, builders, independent investigators.

## Four things you need to care about

1. **What you can trust now** — every conclusion carries its scope, level and evidence.
2. **What was refuted and what is unclear** — refuted judgements stay; unclear ones say so.
3. **What it all adds up to** — the ontology graph and the entity graph.
4. **What it needs from you** — only decisions only you can make.

Everything else about the process appears only when you ask.

## What is different

Most AI products optimise for fluent answers or task completion. ClearAI organises work around **the state of knowledge**: which judgements are under test, on what grounds, within what scope, and what is still unclear. It does only what the host cannot — the epistemic contract, the domain ontology, presentation — and uses DSH's own workspace, goal continuation, subagents, deliverables and file history.

## Boundaries

- It supports inquiry; it does not guarantee truth or make high-stakes decisions for you.
- It can search, analyse, test and organise evidence; it cannot turn missing evidence into certainty.
- Judgement, approval, interpretation and consequential action remain with people.
- No claim of RSI, consciousness, independent agency or unconstrained autonomy.
